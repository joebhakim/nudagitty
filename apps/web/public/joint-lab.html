Three prototypes for controlling the joint while pinning the truth — the design questions from the SCM top-down pass. Every slider moves a nuisance direction; the causal mechanism (the estimand) is held fixed unless the demo is specifically about when it can't be.

① Effect modification untethers the truth

The clean claim "vary the joint, pin the truth" holds for the CATE — τ(C) is a property of the mechanism. But the marginal ATE is ∫τ(C)dP(C), so with effect modification, changing the population mix moves the "average effect" even though no CATE changed. The DGM knob and the target-population knob are entangled.

② One dial: from identified to hopeless

Enriching the noise dependence isn't just "harder" — it crosses qualitative boundaries. Observed confounding worsens overlap (identified, but IPW gets fragile). Unmeasured confounding — a bidirected edge — pushes the estimand out of the identified region: even the correctly-adjusted estimate is biased. Two readings of one landscape. True effect β = 1, adjusting for the observed confounder only.

③ You set the noise, you observe its shadow

You control the copula (a latent correlation r); what you observe is its pushforward through each marginal. For skewed / heavy-tailed marginals the observed Pearson correlation drifts away from r, while rank (Spearman) dependence is preserved. So "specify the correlation matrix" hides an inversion — the NORTA correction — and this is only the root joint; anything downstream is a further pushforward you don't set directly.
