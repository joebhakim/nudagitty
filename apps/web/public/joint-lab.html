Copula Editor

Dependence, decoupled from the marginals. The atomic unit is a bivariate cell; three or more variables are a vine — a cascade of those cells, some conditional. Everything lives in rank space [0,1], so you're editing pure dependence and previewing it live.

① The bivariate cell

Pick a family, set the strength as Kendall's τ (family-invariant, so switching family keeps the same rank-correlation), rotate the Archimedeans to move the tail. τ runs the full [−1, 1] — the ends are the comonotone / countermonotone Fréchet bounds. Gaussian and Frank span both signs; Clayton and Gumbel are positive-tail families, so rotate 90°/270° for negative dependence. The left preview is the pure copula density on the unit square; the right is a sample.

② The 3-variable vine

Three variables aren't a bag of three pairwise copulas (that's over-constrained). They're a cascade: two direct pairs along a chain, plus one conditional pair (the outer two, given the middle). Edit those three cells; the pairwise scatters below are computed. Turn up the dashed conditional cell and watch the outer pair's dependence rise beyond what merely leaks through the middle.
