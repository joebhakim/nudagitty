  • Peters, Janzing & Schölkopf — Elements of Causal Inference MIT Press, 2017

Copulas — the new machinery

Sklar makes (marginals × copula) a lossless coordinate system for any joint.

  • Nelsen — An Introduction to Copulas Springer, 2nd ed. 2006
    The entry point: Sklar's theorem, Fréchet–Hoeffding bounds, Archimedean families.
  • Joe — Dependence Modeling with Copulas CRC, 2014
    The comprehensive reference — tail dependence, multivariate families, vines. (1997 Multivariate Models and Dependence Concepts is the older classic.)
  • Sklar — Fonctions de répartition à n dimensions et leurs marges 1959
    The two-page origin of the whole idea.
  • Genest & Nešlehová — A primer on copulas for count data ASTIN Bulletin, 2007
    The discrete-margin caveat: non-uniqueness and squeezed dependence for binary/count variables. Read before trusting "set any correlation."
    discrete margins

Vines — the actual superset

Pair-copula constructions: any bivariate family per edge along a nested tree sequence.

  • Czado — Analyzing Dependent Data with Vine Copulas Springer, 2019
    The textbook — R-/C-/D-vines, the simplified-vine assumption.
  • Aas, Czado, Frigessi & Bakken — Pair-copula constructions of multiple dependence Insurance: Math. & Econ., 2009
    The foundational vine paper.
  • Bedford & Cooke — Vines: a new graphical model for dependent random variables Annals of Statistics, 2002
    Where vine-as-graph comes from — directly the "bidirected edge = pair-copula" connection.
    copula editor

The bidirected / latent layer — ADMGs

Where "confounding = coupled noise = bidirected edge" lives formally.

  • Pearl — Causality: Models, Reasoning, and Inference 2nd ed., 2009
    SCMs, do-calculus, identification — the non-identified boundary in Demo ②.
    ② sweep
  • Richardson — Markov properties for acyclic directed mixed graphs Scand. J. Statistics, 2003

One honest note: the copula × causal intersection is thin — most copula work is predictive, most causal work treats dependence implicitly through the DAG. The bridge here (a vine on the exogenous noise, under an explicit f) is closer to a design contribution than a textbook chapter. Peters–Janzing–Schölkopf + Joe/Czado are the two halves; the join is the interesting part.
